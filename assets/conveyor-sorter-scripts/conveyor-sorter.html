Sorting SOP Guide

QC Sorting Directives

Tokens bearing Alphabets and Numbers arrive on the moving conveyor. Drag them or use the on-screen buttons to direct each token into its designated containment chamber.

1. Drag & Drop Tokens

Touch and drag any token directly from the moving conveyor into the top chambers. Smooth touch lift ensures high visibility!

2. Dual Chambers

🔤 Alphabets (A, B, C...) go into the Cyan Left Chamber.
🔢 Numbers (1, 2, 3...) go into the Purple Right Chamber.

3. Instant On-Screen Keys

On mobile, tap 🔤 DROP IN ALPHABETS or 🔢 DROP IN NUMBERS to immediately sort the leading token!

4. Streaks & Combos

Consecutive correct sorts earn streak multipliers! Prevent tokens from sliding past the right edge without sorting.
